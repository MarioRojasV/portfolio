Clay-colored Thrush vs female Great-tailed Grackle

Two visually similar Costa Rican birds: Turdus grayi (yigüirro) and the female Quiscalus mexicanus (zanate). Upload a photo and a MobileNetV2 model, developed for COMPDES 2026, tells you which one it thinks it is.

Limitations

  • The model only knows two classes. Any other bird (or object) will still be forced into one of them.
  • It was trained on female grackles only, so males will not be classified reliably.
  • It performs noticeably worse on in-flight photos than on regular photos.

About the model

MobileNetV2 with transfer learning (frozen ImageNet base, 224×224 input). On the held-out test set it reached 96.25% accuracy on regular photos (80 images) and 75% on in-flight photos (20 images, a small sample).

Everything runs in your browser with TensorFlow.js: your photo is not uploaded anywhere. Code and training notebooks on GitHub · Back to portfolio
